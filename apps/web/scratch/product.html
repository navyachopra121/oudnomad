An extract of 99 types of flower oils will take you on a journey to the gardens of heaven (Jannat) with the first wisp. Made from the world’s finest roses sourced from Grasse (France), which bloom for only 30 minutes a year, the fragrance also features Iranian Taif Rose, Jasmine de Grasse, lilies from the French Highlands, and Bulgarian Rose. It takes 700 kilograms of flowers to produce just 1 kilogram of pure flower oil (Attar), which is used in this exquisite perfume. The scent offers a lasting impression with a longevity of up to 48 hours.
